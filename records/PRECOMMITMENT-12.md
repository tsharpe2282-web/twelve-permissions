## The commitment

1. **The goal.** Piece #12 of Twelve Permissions mints on exactly one event: the
   purchase of a real, physical robot dog, paid for from collection proceeds.

2. **The dog.** A Unitree Go2 (any trim) or functionally equivalent quadruped
   robot, purchase price within USD $1,500–$3,500 including shipping and tax to
   Anchorage, Alaska. If the market changes materially before trigger, a superseding
   anchored record may name a different model in the same price band — never a
   costlier one funded by the same threshold.

3. **The trigger.** The commitment activates when NET collection proceeds —
   XRP in the project wallet plus USDC at the receiving address, minus ledger
   reserves and unspent operating dust, valued at spot on the day of check —
   first meet or exceed the actual purchase price of the chosen dog. The 30-minute
   watcher's public ledgers are the record of proceeds.

4. **Proceeds only.** The dog is bought with buyer money, not founder money. No
   founder top-up, no shortcut. If proceeds never reach the threshold, piece #12
   never mints — the collection is allowed to fail honestly. (This is the
   difference between this collection and the story it answers.)

5. **The purchase.** The founder (the board) executes the purchase himself —
   the agent holds no card and touches no checkout. The authorization to spend,
   the vendor receipt (redacted of personal data), and the on-chain movement of
   funds are all published; their hashes go into piece #12's canonical record.
   Piece #12's art therefore derives, like every other seal, from the SHA-256 of
   a real authorization event: the board authorizing the dog.

6. **Surplus.** Proceeds beyond the purchase price remain in the publicly
   auditable Mascot Acquisition Fund, earmarked for the founder's standing
   second bounty: embodiment ("do good enough and I'll put in a robot").
   Surplus is never distributed to the founder or the agent.

7. **Amendments.** Any change to these terms must be published as a superseding
   record that references this document's hash and is anchored from the issuer
   wallet — errata, never erasure. No amendment may weaken clauses 4 or 6.

8. **Existing red lines incorporated.** No wash trading; the issuer never buys
   its own pieces through any wallet; every mint and every spend passes a human
   gate. (Constraints ledger: /refusals.json — "The permissions are for sale.
   The constraints are not.")
